From the Field to the Boardroom

Building a commercial coatings business isn't just about putting product on a roof. It's about building a machine that can estimate accurately, sell confidently, and execute flawlessly without you standing on every job site. I learned that the hard way.

"The gap between a $1M contractor and a $10M enterprise isn't better coating—it's better systems and high-authority advising."

After years of running a successful contracting firm and seeing the lack of true, actionable mentorship in the commercial roofing space, I partnered with the Contractors Choice Agency to build this coaching program. We don't teach theory. We implement the exact frameworks that took my business from a struggling local crew to a regional powerhouse.

Whether you are struggling with closing commercial facility managers, dialing in your estimating spreadsheets, or building a crew structure that scales, I've been there. My mission now is to hand you the blueprint.
