Common Questions

Everything you need to know before we speak.

How long is the call? expand_more
The strategy call is scheduled for 45 minutes. This gives us enough time to dig into your current operational challenges and outline a practical roadmap without wasting your day.
Is this a high-pressure sales pitch? expand_more
Absolutely not. We operate on a contractor-to-contractor basis. The goal of this call is to determine if our methodology is a good fit for your business. If it is, we'll discuss next steps. If not, you walk away with actionable advice.
Do I need to be a certain size to qualify? expand_more
Our systems are designed for commercial coating contractors who are actively operating and looking to scale beyond owner-operator bottlenecks. While there is no strict revenue minimum, you should have an established business structure.
What should I prepare for the call? expand_more
Come prepared with a rough idea of your annual volume, your typical job size, and the primary area where you feel your operation is getting stuck (e.g., getting leads, closing deals, or crew efficiency).
