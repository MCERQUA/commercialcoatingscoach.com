What is the typical format of the coaching? expand_more

We combine 1-on-1 strategic consulting with group mastermind sessions. You will receive customized action plans for your specific market, alongside access to our proprietary systems, document templates, and peer network.

How quickly can I expect to see ROI? expand_more

While results vary based on your implementation speed, many contractors see a return on their investment within the first 60 days by utilizing our precision estimating tools to correct underbid projects and closing larger commercial contracts.
