# Conversion Patterns — commercialcoatingscoach.com (B2B coaching offer)

Distinct from CCA's homeowner/repair-job lead-gen sites: the conversion unit here is a coaching relationship, not a quoted job. Design and copy should follow B2B consulting conversion norms, not home-service CRO norms.

## Primary CTA
"Book a Free Strategy Call" (not "Get a Quote"). Secondary CTA: "Apply for Coaching" on program pages once the visitor is warmer. Phone number still present for trust/legitimacy (canonical CCA number), but the call-booking CTA is dominant, not a "call now, emergency" framing.

## Trust signals (in priority order)
1. Founder/coach credibility — real trade background (CCA's former-contractor, 20+ years framing), not anonymous "our team."
2. Case-study proof — described in terms of the SYSTEM implemented and the outcome pattern (e.g. "bid win-rate improved after we fixed the labor-rate model"), not fabricated named-client dollar figures we can't verify. Per hard rule: no fabricated results attributed to named clients.
3. Program specificity — proof this is built for coatings/roofing, not generic "any contractor" coaching.
4. No urgency/scarcity tactics — no countdown timers, no "only 3 spots left." Calm authority framing throughout.

## Lead form
Since this is a consultative B2B offer, NOT a 4-field home-service repair form: Name, Phone, Email, Company Name, and a short "what's the biggest challenge in your business right now?" dropdown or short-text field are appropriate — this is closer to a "book a call" qualification form than an emergency-repair lead form. Still keep it short (5-6 fields max) and still wire AEO traffic_source/landing_url hidden fields and the Netlify static-export pattern per the platform standard.

## Section rhythm implication
Homepage should include a "results framework" band (inverted dark) explaining HOW the coaching creates results (systems-based, not hype-based) rather than a home-service "trust bar" of star ratings/review counts, since this audience is skeptical of generic coaching hype and responds better to a credible operating framework.
