Referrals feel free, which is exactly why most commercial coatings contractors never build anything else. The problem shows up later: referral-only growth is capped at the size of the owner's existing network, and that ceiling gets more visible every year revenue tries to grow past it.

## Who Actually Buys Commercial Coatings Work

Before any marketing system makes sense, the buyer has to be specific. For most commercial and industrial coatings contractors, that's facility managers, property managers, and general contractors — not homeowners, and not a generic "business owner" persona borrowed from a marketing course built for a different industry.

## Why Generic Ad Spend Underperforms

"Get more leads" ad campaigns without a defined ideal-customer target tend to attract the wrong volume — clicks and form-fills that don't convert to booked commercial jobs. Spend without a target buyer isn't a marketing system, it's a bet, and it's usually an expensive one.

## What a Basic, Consistent System Looks Like

- **Trade-specific content and outreach** aimed directly at facility managers and property managers, not general homeowner content repurposed for a commercial audience.
- **A referral system with structure**, not just hoping past clients remember to mention you — a simple, consistent ask built into the project close-out process.
- **Lead-source tracking**, so you actually know which channel is producing booked jobs versus which one just produces inquiries that go nowhere.
- **A budget and cadence sized to your company**, not a marketing agency's standard retainer built for a bigger business.

## It Doesn't Have to Be Expensive to Work

The goal isn't matching a national brand's ad spend — it's removing the ceiling that referral-only growth creates. A basic, consistent system aimed at the right buyer type outperforms a bigger budget aimed at the wrong one.
